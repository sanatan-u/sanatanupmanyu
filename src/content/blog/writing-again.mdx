After years of building AI platforms behind NDAs and speaking about the outcomes at conferences, I'm going to start writing here.

I care about the messy middle — the part between "we should use AI for this" and "the trial finished six months early." Most of what I've shipped lives in that middle: knowledge graphs over messy literature, patient digital twins that look nothing like real patients, portfolio intelligence that has to survive a BD team's scrutiny on Monday morning.

You'll see notes on:

- How we actually wire up agentic workflows that clinicians will trust
- Where digital twins help, and the three places they mislead
- What I'm learning building [QuriousRI](https://app.quriousri.com) and [AlphaMeld](https://alphameld.com)
- Talks I'm preparing, papers I'm reading, decisions I'm second-guessing

Short posts. No listicles. Occasional diagrams.
